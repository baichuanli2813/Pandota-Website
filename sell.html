Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will I get paid?

Once your laptop arrives at our Birmingham facility, our technicians conduct a swift hardware inspection within 24 hours. Upon verification, payment is issued immediately via UK Faster Payments directly into your bank account.

How do I ship my laptop safely?

We recommend using an insured, tracked courier service (such as Royal Mail Special Delivery or DPD). Pack the laptop securely with plenty of bubble wrap inside its original box or a sturdy protective carton.

Do you buy faulty or cosmetic-damaged units?

Yes! We frequently buy machines with minor cosmetic imperfections, battery wear, or repairable faults. Please select "For Parts or Faulty" in the quote form and describe any known issues.

What about data security on my SSD?

We take customer data privacy seriously. All storage drives undergo certified military-grade data sanitization and wiping upon receipt, ensuring zero remnants of personal data remain.

Can you buy bulk lots or liquidation pallets?

Absolutely. We regularly purchase corporate liquidation manifests, customer return pallets, and excess distributor inventory from 5 to 100+ units. Email manifests to purchasing@pandota.co.uk.

Can I trade in against an item in stock?

Yes! We offer enhanced trade-in valuations if you wish to put your current machine's value towards any high-performance laptop listed in our active inventory.
